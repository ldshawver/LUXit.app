What’s inside

Built around the customer, not the channel

Know who you’re talking to

Unified contacts with the full customer history — messages, calls, notes and activity — plus identity resolution that keeps duplicates from splintering the record.

  • One contact record across every channel
  • Conversation and call history on the timeline
  • Identity resolution and duplicate prevention
  • Contact import from CSV and connected sources
  • Notes, tags and activity tracking

Segment your customers

Build audiences from your customer data, preview exactly who is included, and reuse the same definitions across every campaign. Preview and send apply the same rules.

  • Audiences from tags, activity and customer attributes
  • Preview the exact audience before you send
  • Ineligible and opted-out contacts excluded automatically
  • Same audience rules in preview and execution
  • Tenant-scoped — segments never cross customers

Message with consent built in

Send SMS to the customers who opted in. Promotional consent, opt-in workflows and STOP/opt-out handling are part of the send path, not an afterthought.

  • Promotional consent and opt-in workflows
  • STOP / opt-out enforced on every send
  • Consent-aware messaging controls and audience eligibility
  • Delivery and consent history on the customer record
  • Twilio-backed SMS delivery

Call from the browser — LUX Connect

Inbound and outbound calling from LUXit and its mobile PWA, with the customer’s identity and history on screen and recent calls logged to the record.

  • Browser and mobile-PWA calling — no desk phone
  • Inbound and outbound on your business number
  • Caller identity and customer context on the call screen
  • Recent calls and voicemail on the customer timeline
