How it works

One connected customer workflow

Each step uses what the last one learned: Know → Segment → Reach → Talk → Follow Up → Grow.

What’s inside

Built around the customer, not the channel

Know who you’re talking to

Unified contacts with the full customer history — messages, calls, notes and activity — plus identity resolution that keeps duplicates from splintering the record.

Segment your customers

Build audiences from your customer data, preview exactly who is included, and reuse the same definitions across every campaign. Preview and send apply the same rules.

Message with consent built in

Send SMS to the customers who opted in. Promotional consent, opt-in workflows and STOP/opt-out handling are part of the send path, not an afterthought. It gives you consent-aware messaging controls — you remain responsible for your own compliance obligations.

Call from the browser — LUX Connect

Inbound and outbound calling from LUXit and its mobile PWA, with the customer’s identity and history on screen and recent calls logged to the record. See the LUX Connect page →
